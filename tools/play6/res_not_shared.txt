# Resource files of apps/Z9xProjector that the Play 6 projector app does not share (docs/play6/SETTINGS_PLAN.md
# 2.4). Every file of apps/Z9xProjector/res is either listed in apps/common/projector/FILES (shared) or matches
# a pattern here; tools/play6/check_forks.py (check F) fails otherwise, and on a pattern that matches nothing.
# Format: pattern (relative to apps/Z9xProjector, shell glob, values* covers the 17 locale copies) <TAB> reason
res/values*/strings_kst.xml	optical zoom texts of the Z9X 1.0.2 lane; Play 6 keystone labels go to strings_p6.xml
res/values*/strings_sound.xml	Z9X sound chain texts; Play 6 sound rows have their own text in strings_p6.xml
res/values*/strings_game.xml	Z9X game speed and console texts; Play 6 game rows have their own text in strings_p6.xml
res/values*/strings_ak.xml	decided when the AK overlay is ported (P2)
res/values*/strings_cec.xml	decided when the CEC page is ported (P2)
res/values*/strings_dream.xml	decided when the dreams are ported (P2)
res/values*/strings_display.xml	3D and UI resolution are SKIP on the Play 6
res/values*/strings_report.xml	problem reports are SKIP on the Play 6
res/values/strings_sys.xml	only sys_placeholder, unused
res/drawable/ic_eye_mask.xml	the Play 6 has its own copy since the eye protection port (2026-10-21, forks.tsv)
res/drawable/brand_icon_projector.xml	single source apps/common/brand (brand/sync.sh), not in FILES
res/drawable/brand_banner_projector.xml	single source apps/common/brand (brand/sync.sh), not in FILES
res/drawable/banner.xml	not referenced by the Z9X code or manifest
res/drawable/ic_projector.xml	not referenced by the Z9X code or manifest
res/drawable/notice_bg.xml	not referenced by the Z9X code or manifest
res/drawable/ic_tile_manual_keystone.xml	not referenced by the Z9X code or manifest
res/xml/dream_clock.xml	dreams (P2)
res/values*/strings_pro.xml	Z9X 1.0.4 professional picture page; the Play 6 picture rows have their own text in strings_p6.xml
res/values*/strings_stock.xml	Z9X 1.0.4 stock features (HDMI, zoom and shift, rotation, power on, guide); Play 6 rows have their own text in strings_p6.xml
